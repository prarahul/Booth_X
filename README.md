# Campaign Command Heart

Create the complete web application and marketing platform for Booth X, a political tech startup dealing with booth management, voter mobilization, ground mobile apps, and electoral data analytics integration. Follow and elevate the logic, structure, and features shown in the attached screenshots:

1. Navigation & Brand:
   - Modern, authoritative branding for "Booth X" with high-stakes war-room aesthetics (sleek dark slate / midnight blue, vibrant amber and emerald indicators, clean cards).
   - Navigation: About, Services, Research, Intelligence, DataGrid, Why Us, Contact.
   - Quick actions: "Get Mobile App", "Partner Login", and "Book Consultation" CTA.

2. Hero Section:
   - Tagline: "Political Campaign Strategy & Intelligence".
   - Compelling headline: "Shaping voices that win elections. Booth by booth."
   - Primary and secondary CTAs: "Book a Consultation" and "Explore Platform".
   - Interactive hero preview: A simulated Booth X live feed showing real-time voter entries, swing booth status, and winnable booth flags.
   - 4-pillar quick strip: Research (Know every booth), Ground (Mobilize every voter), Digital (Own the narrative), AI (Answers on your phone).

3. About & Core Philosophy:
   - "Politics is won on the ground. We make sure you own that ground."
   - 3 pillars: Ground Truth First, Technology That Serves the Field, Complete Discretion & NDA Protection.

4. 9 Comprehensive Services:
   - Research & Survey (demographics, swing booths, issues, influencers)
   - Ground Campaign & Panna Pramukh Mobilization (booth committees, yatras, public events)
   - Digital & Social Media Strategy (unified narrative across WhatsApp, X, FB, YouTube)
   - Creative & Video Production (documentaries, ad films, AI news anchors)
   - Voter & Electoral Data Analysis (rolls, past trends, caste/community composition)
   - Booth X Intelligence & AI Strategist (real-time mobile assistant & war room dashboard)
   - Booth X DataGrid (converting raw PDF rolls into searchable tabular datasets)
   - Events & Outreach Programs (end-to-end community programs)
   - Mass Communication (bulk WhatsApp, AI personalized voice/video, LED vans)

5. Booth-Level Research & Analytics Section:
   - "Know exactly where you stand — booth by booth."
   - Deliverables checklist: voter composition, party leanings & swings, key issues, vote influencers, and booth classifications (Stronghold, Winnable, Swing, Tough).
   - Interactive data preview: Demographic bars (OBC, General, SC, Minority, etc.), party leaning matrix, and booth count cards with filters.
   - Dedicated highlight on women voter mobilization.

6. Booth X Intelligence & AI Assistant Simulator:
   - "Your constituency, one question away."
   - Mobile dashboard preview with live updates, constituency strength metrics, and panchayat/ward drill-down.
   - Interactive conversational AI demo where users can click sample questions (in English and Hindi) to see instant intelligent strategic responses.

7. Booth X DataGrid (PDF-to-Intelligence Engine):
   - "From PDF to Political Intelligence."
   - Step-by-step pipeline: Upload -> AI Processing -> Structured Data -> Search & Filter.
   - "Before vs. With DataGrid" comparative card.
   - Interactive table preview with simulated voter records (Booth, Section, Name, Age, Gender, Leaning) and search/filter controls.
   - Built for: Campaign war rooms, political consultants, election strategists, data teams.

8. "Why Leaders Choose Booth X":
   - 6 key trust pillars: Booth-level clarity, Unique political AI, Unified ground-to-digital team, Women at the centre, Real-time accountability, and Air-gapped confidentiality.

9. Conversion & Contact Section:
   - High-impact consultation banner: "Know every booth. Win every booth."
   - Office details, phone/WhatsApp, email, and social handles.
   - "Tell us about your campaign" callback request form.
   - Full footer with legal links (Privacy Policy & Terms of Use pages included).
